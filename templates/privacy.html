Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Your financial information is personal. This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly ("we", "the Service") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

1. Data We Collect

We collect only what is needed to run the Service:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and a securely hashed password.
  • Expense data — the amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter.
  • Technical data — basic session cookies required to keep you signed in.

We do not collect bank account numbers, card details, or connect to your financial institutions.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your information is used to:

  • Create and secure your account.
  • Store, display, and summarise your expenses.
  • Generate category breakdowns and spending reports for you.
  • Respond to support requests you send us.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use your expense records for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in a secured database. Passwords are never stored in plain text. While we take reasonable measures to protect your information, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. You may delete your account at any time, after which your personal and expense data will be removed.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal or expense data with third parties, except where required by law. Pages may load web fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive basic technical information such as your IP address in order to deliver those files.

5. Your Rights

You may access, correct, export, or delete the information in your account at any time. If you need help exercising these rights, contact us using the details below.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Please also review our Terms and Conditions.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out through the contact details provided within the app.
